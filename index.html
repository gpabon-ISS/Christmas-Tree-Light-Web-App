1 · Song & starting point

Auto-choreographer

Listens to the whole song and finds every peak — each note hit and every jump in volume — plus every drop. Peaks get an effect fired right on the hit (busier sections get more of them), the biggest moments in the song get the accent and a full-brightness flash, and the tree's brightness follows the music: it swells with the music and dims the moment the volume falls. Effects change with the song's sections, and repeated sections get the same look. Classic works like GE's own shows instead: one effect retriggered in time with the melody for a run of about 8 hits, then the next effect, with an occasional flash between runs and no brightness changes.

Load a song into the editor first.

Preview

—

A stand-in for the tree: it shows the current color and effect, not the exact animation.

Flash with music

Listens to the microphone (or a song playing in this page) and fires effects on the beat, like the app's music mode.

Send raw bytes

Every command is 10 bytes starting with FF. Byte 2 is the command; the rest are parameters. Colors are 0–F per channel in B G R order.

Protocol (decoded from the Android app)

BytesMeaning
FF 28 FE B G R 00 00 00 00Solid color, not saved (B/G/R = 0–F)
FF 18 FE B G R 00 00 00 00Solid color, saved as default
FF 04 LL SS 00 …Brightness LL (1–100); SS=01 preview, 00 save
FF 10 … FF 1E, FF 30Built-in effects (param in byte 3 on some)
FF 21 … FF 27Patterns
FF 01 DD B G R …Pop color (other models)
FF 03 CC B G R / FF 05 RR B G RColumn / row effects (other models)

Connection: Bluetooth Classic serial (SPP). Built-in shows: the app plays the MP3 and sends a cue whenever playback is within 100 ms of a table entry.
